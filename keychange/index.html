Monophony listening check

Part of keychange, a study of key-scale estimation and degree-wise key-scale conversion for single-track loops in a DAW. The conversion path assumes a monophonic track: one pitch at a time. FSL10K loops are filtered with an automatic check (share of voiced frames and pitch-tracker confidence) whose thresholds have not been validated. These 20 loops are a random, shuffled mix of loops the check accepted and rejected; which is which is not shown, so the judgement stays blind.

How to judge

  • mono: one pitched line at a time. Legato overlaps, reverb tails, slides and vibrato are fine.
  • poly: two or more pitches sound together for a noticeable part of the loop: chords, octave doubling, stacked or layered voices, harmonised lines, a pad under the line.
  • unclear: cannot tell, or the loop is mostly unpitched (noise, percussion).

Choices are kept in this browser. When finished, press Download CSV and send the file back.
